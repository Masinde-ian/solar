Our Story

Powering Kenya Since 2016

Founded in 2016, SunPro has grown from a small workshop in Nairobi to become one of Kenya's most trusted solar energy providers. We are EPRA licensed (Tier-3) and have completed over 500 installations across Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru and across the country.

Our mission is simple: make reliable solar energy accessible and affordable for every Kenyan home and business. We partner with global leaders like Jinko Solar, Victron Energy, and Huawei to deliver premium systems backed by comprehensive warranties. From free site assessment to professional installation and lifetime support, we are with you every step of the way.
