FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do solar panels work when it's cloudy in Nairobi?+
Yes! Our high-efficiency Monocrystalline panels (22%+ efficiency) generate 40–70% of rated power even in low-light conditions. Nairobi still averages 5.5 peak sun hours per day even in the rainy season.
How much does a solar panel cost in Kenya?+
A quality 550W Monocrystalline panel starts from Ksh 22,000. A complete installed home system ranges from Ksh 85,000 (basic backup) to Ksh 850,000+ (full off-grid). We provide free quotes — contact us via WhatsApp.
How long does installation take?+
Most residential installations are completed in 1–2 days. Commercial systems (50kW+) may take 3–7 days. We schedule at your convenience and cause minimal disruption to your daily routine.
Do you offer warranty on installations?+
Yes. Solar panels come with a 25-year performance warranty. Inverters carry 5-year warranties. Batteries (LiFePO4) have 10-year warranties. Our workmanship is warranted for 2 years after installation.
Can I add more panels or batteries later?+
Absolutely. We design all systems to be expandable. Starting small and upgrading later is a smart approach for many of our customers as their energy needs grow.
